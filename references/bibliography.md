# Bibliography

Links only. No PDFs are committed. All of Oziewicz's papers are available on arXiv
or ResearchGate as well as on publishers' sites; ResearchGate links are added
as they are collected. The "Checked" column says what was compared against the
arXiv record on 31 October 2026; anything not listed there is taken from Bill
Page's earlier bibliography file and has not been re-verified.
