OK, next was an electrical engineer, Mocanu, in Bucharest, in 1985. He considered the transformation of the electric field from one reference system to another according to the formulas that you can find in any book of electrodynamics; in Jackson's book, for example, you have the formulas for how to transform the electric field and magnetic field from one reference system to another. He did this twice: he transformed the electromagnetic field from reference system A to reference system B, and then, with another velocity, to reference system C. And he found the following. Einstein, in his main paper, assumes that if B has velocity u with respect to A, then A must have velocity minus u with respect to B. This was a trivial assumption, called the reciprocity assumption. But this is the most important assumption of Einstein's theory, not the velocity of light! It is exactly this assumption that contradicts the relativity of space, as I will explain later.
