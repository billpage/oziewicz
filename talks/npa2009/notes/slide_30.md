So we believe that physics is a description in terms of tensors, because tensors do not know what coordinate system you are using, and a coordinate system obscures the situation. So there are two points of view. One is that coordinates and bases belong to mathematics: they are only conventions, and physics must be described in terms of scalars, vector fields, tensors, differential forms, Grassmann multiforms and so on. For example, if you change Cartesian coordinates to polar coordinates on the plane, you are not changing the plane; you are not changing anything. In some coordinates something is more convenient, and in other coordinates something else is more convenient. So coordinates are not so relevant for the description of physics.
