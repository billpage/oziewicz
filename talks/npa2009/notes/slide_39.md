So what is going on? Einstein's velocity is in a factor space, the Lobachevsky factor space. But the Lorentz group has six parameters, and how do you select from six parameters the ones that correspond to the three parameters of relative velocity? You must select some rotation subgroup of the Lorentz group. This is a remark to mathematicians. But of course the Lorentz group has infinitely many rotation subgroups, and each choice of rotation subgroup gives you one relative velocity between the reference systems. This "standard choice" of the subgroup is the same as the choice of a privileged reference system. This is my statement, the main achievement of my life, but nobody listens to me.
