# Notice: material by Zbigniew Oziewicz

This directory and the two narrated pages built from it contain material that
is Zbigniew Oziewicz's, not this repository's:

- the slides of his talk *What is Special Relativity Theory?*, given online to
  the Natural Philosophy Alliance (Beamer PDF dated 29 December 2009), shown as
  images in `npa2009.html` and in both narrated pages;
- the words of the talk, in `notes/` and in the `NOTES` of the pages. The
  notes are a transcript, lightly corrected (see [`README.md`](README.md));
  the words are his;
- his recorded voice, in `npa2009_oziewicz_narrated.html` on the `output`
  branch.

Oziewicz sent the PDF to Bill Page, and the recording was made at the talk.
Oziewicz died in 2020. The slides and the recording are not known to have been
published elsewhere, and no licence from his estate has been obtained. They
are included for the study of his work, with attribution, and are **not**
covered by the licences of this repository (AGPL-3.0-or-later for code,
CC-BY-SA-4.0 for documents). Anyone holding rights in this material who
objects to its inclusion can open an issue, and it will be removed.

Covered by the repository licences: [`README.md`](README.md) (CC-BY-SA-4.0),
`slide_to_pdfpage.json` and `pronunciations.json` (CC-BY-SA-4.0), and
[`src/narration/build_slides.py`](../../src/narration/build_slides.py)
(AGPL-3.0-or-later). The synthetic voice in
`npa2009_andrew_narrated.html` was generated with Microsoft Edge's speech
service (voice `en-US-AndrewNeural`); it reads Oziewicz's words, so that page
falls under this notice as a whole.
