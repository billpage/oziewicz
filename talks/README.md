# Talks

Oziewicz's talks, as narrated slide pages: the slides, an audio player per
slide and the transcript. Each talk has a directory with its notes and a
README; the narrated pages themselves, with the audio embedded, are on the
`output` branch and are served by GitHub Pages once their SHA-256 is listed in
[`narrated.sha256`](narrated.sha256).

The slides, words and recordings are Oziewicz's and are **not** under this
repository's licences; each talk's `NOTICE.md` says what is included and on
what basis.

- **[`npa2009/`](npa2009/README.md)** *What is Special Relativity Theory?*,
  online to the Natural Philosophy Alliance, December 2009: the relativity
  groupoid against the Lorentz group, ternary and binary velocity, and the
  "astonishing conflict" read in
  [`supplement/bodies_first.md`](../supplement/bodies_first.md#6-the-astonishing-conflict)
  §6. In [Oziewicz's voice](https://billpage.github.io/oziewicz/narrated/npa2009_oziewicz_narrated.html)
  and [read by a synthetic voice](https://billpage.github.io/oziewicz/narrated/npa2009_andrew_narrated.html).
